================================================================================
                    OMNIDESK AI / APEX RETAIL STORE POLICIES & FAQ
================================================================================

1. RETURN AND EXCHANGE POLICY
- 30-Day Return Window: Customers may return eligible products within 30 calendar days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.
- Condition Requirement: Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all included accessories, manuals, and warranty cards intact.
- Restocking Fees: Open-box electronics and customized items may incur a 15% restocking fee unless defective upon arrival.
- Return Shipping: Standard return shipping is free for all orders placed in the United States and Canada using our prepaid return labels. For international returns, return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer.
- Refund Processing Time: Once an item arrives at our fulfillment warehouse, please allow 3 to 5 business days for quality inspection. After approval, the refund will post to your bank or card within 5 to 7 business days.

2. SHIPPING AND DELIVERY
- Domestic Shipping (USA):
  * Standard Shipping (3-5 business days): Free on orders over $50; flat rate $4.99 for orders under $50.
  * Expedited 2-Day Shipping: $14.99 flat rate.
  * Overnight Delivery: $29.99 flat rate for orders placed before 1:00 PM EST.
- International Shipping: We ship to over 85 countries. International delivery takes between 7 to 14 business days via DHL Express.
- Customs and Import Duties: All international orders are shipped DDP (Delivered Duty Paid); import taxes and customs duties are calculated and collected at checkout so there are no surprise fees on delivery.
- Order Tracking: Tracking numbers are generated and emailed automatically within 24 hours of package dispatch.

3. ORDER MODIFICATION AND CANCELLATION
- Cancellation Window: Orders can be cancelled or modified within 60 minutes of placement directly from your account dashboard or by contacting live support.
- Post-Window Modifications: After 60 minutes, orders enter automated warehouse picking and cannot be cancelled. Once delivered, you may initiate a standard return.
- Address Updates: If your order has not yet shipped, our customer support team can update your delivery address immediately upon request.

4. WARRANTY AND REPAIR COVERAGE
- 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty: All hardware products include a 1-year limited warranty covering defects in materials and manufacturing workmanship.
- What is NOT Covered: Cosmetic wear-and-tear, accidental drops, water damage (unless specified as waterproof), and unauthorized third-party repairs are not covered under the standard warranty.
- Warranty Claim Process: To initiate a warranty claim, submit your order number, serial number, and a brief description with photos of the issue to support@company.com or via the Support Hub portal. Replacement units are shipped via expedited 2-day delivery once approved.
- Extended Warranty Protection (Apex Care+): Provides 2 additional years of coverage including up to 2 accidental damage incidents per year with a $29 replacement deductible.

5. PAYMENT METHODS, BILLING & PRICE MATCHING
- Accepted Payment Methods: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Klarna / Affirm installment plans (0% APR available for qualified customers).
- Currency & Billing: All transactions are billed in USD unless local currency conversion is selected at checkout.
- 14-Day Price Match Guarantee: If an item you purchased goes on sale or is available at a lower price on an authorized retailer's website within 14 days of purchase, contact support for a refund of the price difference.

6. CUSTOMER SUPPORT ESCALATION & HOURS
- AI Support Hub: Available 24/7/365 with instant answers grounded in our verified knowledge base.
- Live Agent Hours: Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST; Saturday and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST.
- Escalation Email: support@company.com (Average response time: under 2 hours during business hours).
- Phone Support: +1 (800) 555-APEX available for Priority and Enterprise tier members.

7. DAMAGED, LOST OR STOLEN PACKAGES
- Transit Damage Reporting: If your order arrives with visible shipping box or product damage, report the issue within 7 calendar days of delivery via the Visual Claim Inspector or support@company.com with photos of the damaged item and shipping label.
- Lost in Transit: Domestic packages with no tracking movement for 7 consecutive business days (or 14 business days for international DHL Express shipments) are declared lost in transit and qualify for an immediate free replacement or 100% refund.
- Stolen / Porch Piracy Claims: If carrier tracking marks a package as "Delivered" but you have not received it, wait 24 hours for premature carrier scans. After 24 hours, file a Missing Package Affidavit within 10 calendar days of the delivery scan. Orders over $500 require a local police report number for instant replacement dispatch.

8. LOYALTY TIERS, VIP MEMBERSHIP & STUDENT DISCOUNTS
- Standard Retail Tier: Free membership on account creation; includes 30-day returns and standard support queue access.
- Gold VIP Tier ($2,500+ annual spend or $99/year): Includes free Expedited 2-Day Domestic Shipping on all orders, extended 60-day return window, zero restocking fees on open-box electronics, and priority ticket routing (1-hour SLA target).
- Student, Educator & First Responder Discount: Verified students (with a valid .edu email), teachers, healthcare workers, and military personnel receive an extra 10% discount on hardware purchases using promo code STUDENT10 or HERO10 at checkout. Cannot be combined with flash-sale bundles.

9. SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTIONS, LICENSING & DIGITAL REFUNDS
- Cloud & SaaS Subscriptions: Monthly and annual OmniDesk Cloud Pro subscriptions auto-renew at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date in Account Settings -> Billing.
- Digital & License Key Refund Window: Annual software plans cancelled within 14 calendar days of initial purchase or renewal qualify for a full refund. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime to stop future billing; prorated mid-month refunds are not issued.
- License Seat Transfers: Enterprise administrators can reassign active seat licenses between team members unlimited times at no additional cost via the Admin Portal.

10. ACCOUNT SECURITY, PRIVACY & DATA DELETION
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Customers can enable TOTP authenticator apps or passkeys under Account -> Security. Password reset links expire after 15 minutes for security.
- Suspicious Charge Lockout: If you notice unauthorized account activity or unrecognized charges, click "Freeze Account" in the portal or email security@company.com for an immediate 15-minute fraud investigation lock.
- GDPR & CCPA Data Deletion Requests: Customers have the right to export all personal data (JSON/CSV) or request permanent account erasure. Verified deletion requests are completed within 30 calendar days, excluding legally required tax invoice records.

11. TRADE-IN, REFURBISHED HARDWARE & BATTERY RECYCLING
- Hardware Trade-In Program: Customers can trade in eligible laptops, tablets, and audio gear for instant store credit. Once you accept a trade-in valuation online, you have 21 calendar days to ship the device using our free prepaid label.
- Certified Refurbished Products: All Apex Certified Refurbished devices undergo a 42-point hardware diagnostic, receive a brand-new battery and outer shell, and carry the same 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty and 30-day return policy as brand-new items.
- Lithium-Ion Battery Recycling: Send any end-of-life electronics to our recycling center using a free e-waste label from your dashboard and receive a $15 green-reward voucher toward your next order over $100.

12. B2B BULK ORDERS, NET-30 INVOICING & ENTERPRISE SLA
- Volume Discounts: Commercial orders of 10 to 49 units receive an automatic 8% volume discount; 50+ units receive a 15% discount and dedicated account manager assignment.
- Net-30 Corporate Invoicing: Approved business, university, and government procurement accounts can check out via Purchase Order (PO) on Net-30 payment terms. Late balances beyond 30 days incur a 1.5% monthly finance charge.
- Tax-Exempt Purchasing: Upload a valid state Reseller Permit or 501(c)(3) Tax-Exempt Certificate in the Billing Portal for automatic sales tax removal within 4 business hours.
- Enterprise SLA Guarantee: VIP Enterprise accounts receive a 99.9% platform uptime guarantee, 30-minute Urgent Ticket response SLA, and dedicated Slack/Webhook escalation channels.
